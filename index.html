📖 How to Play

Everything you need to rule the drug empire.

🚀 Quick Tutorial

01

Tap to harvest

Tap the big brick to package product. Tap power grows as you buy operations and upgrades. Hold to auto-tap!

02

Buy operations

Operations move product every second — even while you're away. Buy ×1, ×10, ×25 or ×100 and double them with upgrades.

03

Rebuild (⭐)

Reach massive hauls and Rebuild to earn 🗝️ Connections. Connections permanently boost ALL production. Use them for permanent upgrades.

04

Play Merge

The classic stacking game is built in! Press Start, drop matching product to cook bigger stacks. Every merge earns your empire product.

05

Whack-a-Cartel

Whack the thugs that pop up before they vanish. Bosses are worth ×5! Score becomes product and drops.

06

Roulette

Spin for crystals! Win up to 100 💎 or a ×2 merge boost. Costs 10 💎 per spin (discounts reduce it).

07

Quests & drops

Do story + daily contracts for crystals & XP. Clicks, buys, cash drops and bosses drop crates with collectibles. Fill your collection!

08

Level up

Earn XP from everything you do. Every level = more production, more tap power and free crystals.

09

Bosses 👹

Every 45–80 minutes the DEA or a rival attacks! Smash it fast for big crystal + crate rewards. Beat your own record!

10

Global events

Random events hit the whole underworld at once — Cartel Wars, Cash Rains, Product Rushes and more. Tap the banner!

11

Cash drops

A glowing cash bundle appears randomly. Grab it fast — Lucky Jackpot, Frenzy, Pack Storm or Time Warp!

12

Skins & currency

Spend 💎 crystals on skins and permanent shop boosts. Your live balance updates on the board in real time.

❓ FAQ

How do I save my progress?

Automatically — it's saved in your browser's local storage every few seconds. No account needed. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

Where does the leaderboard live?

Every cartel shares one free keyless cloud store (MantleDB), so the board is truly global and updates in real time. Your haul score updates when you buy or spend.

What are Connections and Rebuild?

When you Rebuild you convert your run's product into 🗝️ Connections. Each connection permanently boosts production. It's the core end-game loop.

How do I get crystals 💎?

Daily rewards, achievements, contracts, cash drops, crates, roulette, bosses and merge milestones. Spend them on skins, boosts and permanent shop upgrades.

What is the Cartel tab for?

Hire personnel (lookouts, sicarios, soldiers, hitmen, capos…) and buy weapons. Your army raises ⚔️ raid power and 🛡️ defense and every soldier adds a production bonus.

How does the merge mini game help my empire?

Every merge adds product to your empire, big merges drop rare collectibles, and making a Narco King grants a legendary drop + crystals.

What are the bosses?

A DEA raid or rival cartel attacks randomly every 45–80 minutes. Tap it to deal damage before the timer runs out. Defeat it for crystals, crates and XP.

What are the global events?

Random timed events (Cartel War, Cash Rain, Product Rush, Coke Rush, Gold Rush) affect the whole underworld at once. Tap the banner when it appears.
